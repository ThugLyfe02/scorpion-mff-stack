## Why this matters more than a table of MFE

The stack's exit assumption is that his posted level was attainable. This data **cannot confirm or refute that for most trades**, because the move happened before the first bar. Saying so is the result: an assumption labelled *unverified* is safer than one averaged over a window that does not contain it. What the bars *do* show is the cost of carrying: what the position was worth once regular hours opened, and how many contracts finished at ~zero.
